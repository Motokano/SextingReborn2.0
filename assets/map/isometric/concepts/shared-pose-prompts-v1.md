# 共用姿势设计图提示词

工具：内置 ImageGen。输出为姿势参考整图，非生产模块贴图。

## 支撑姿势

Design a LANDSCAPE production concept sheet, exactly SIX equal panels 3 columns x 2 rows, generous gutters, consistent scale and fixed camera. Use reference ONLY for character identity/art style: cartoon boardgame pawn, short black hair, cream blank face, grey sweater blue collar and cuffs, charcoal pants and black shoes, thick clean dark outlines, muted painted texture. Same wide thin oval grey pedestal under every pose, same size, whole figure stays on pedestal. Actual small articulated toy proportions, about 3 heads tall. This sheet deliberately REPLACES the reference's injury rules: disabled arms/legs are COMPLETELY ABSENT as detachable toy modules, clean sealed joints, no gore, no stumps resembling wounds, no floating pieces, no bandages. Head chest abdomen ALWAYS present. All figures face viewer, character's RIGHT is on VIEWER LEFT. Only use panel labels P0 P1 P2 P3 P4 P5, no other text. P0 top-left: NORMAL standing, exactly two arms and two legs. P1 top-middle: character LEFT leg ABSENT (viewer right leg absent); only character RIGHT leg (viewer left) exists and bends deeply supporting a lowered torso, TWO ARMS present, one hand planted on pedestal to stabilize. No second trouser leg or second shoe. P2 top-right: BOTH ARMS ABSENT and LEFT LEG ABSENT; only one right leg remains. Low side-seated pose with lower torso touching pedestal, one bent leg extending on viewer-left. No arms or hands whatsoever, no second leg. P3 bottom-left: BOTH LEGS COMPLETELY ABSENT, exactly two arms. Torso seated directly on pedestal, two hands brace on pedestal at its sides, no trousers legs or shoes. P4 bottom-middle: BOTH LEGS ABSENT and LEFT ARM ABSENT; only RIGHT ARM on viewer-left exists. Lower torso seated on pedestal, leaning onto sole right hand planted at viewer-left. No other arm, no legs or shoes. P5 bottom-right: ALL FOUR LIMBS ABSENT; only head, chest, abdomen. Torso lies on its side DIRECTLY ON pedestal, head tilted alongside torso; not standing, no hands no arms no feet no legs. Keep hip/abdomen as body bottom without leg modules. Pale warm grey background, no alarming content, no blood. Modest short shadow to lower right under every figure. Strictly count visible limbs for each panel. Do not invent missing limbs for balance. Show stable support from remaining modules or body contact. Crisp professionally arranged reference art.

## 混合缺失与躯干伤态

Create a wide landscape reference sheet EXACTLY EIGHT panels, FOUR columns TWO rows. Match attached pawn design exactly: compact cartoon office-worker toy on thin oval grey pedestal, black short hair, blank cream face, grey sweater pale blue collar/cuffs/hem, charcoal legs black shoes. Near FRONTAL fixed camera. Each panel only label M1 M2 M3 M4 T1 T2 T3 T4, no other words. Same scale, pedestal size and muted painted dark-outline style, pale warm grey background. TOP ROW tests the FOUR distinct combinations with exactly ONE ARM and ONE LEG remaining. Missing modules COMPLETELY ABSENT, clean toy body outline no dangling sleeves/no extra limb/no wound/no gore. Body is low, lower abdomen may sit on pedestal for support. Screen positions MUST be followed: M1: ONLY arm on VIEWER LEFT and ONLY leg on VIEWER LEFT; viewer-right side has neither arm nor leg. One hand on viewer-left touches pedestal, single bent leg on viewer-left. M2: ONLY arm on VIEWER LEFT, ONLY leg on VIEWER RIGHT. Hand and shoe touch pedestal at opposite sides; NO viewer-right arm NO viewer-left leg. M3: ONLY arm on VIEWER RIGHT, ONLY leg on VIEWER LEFT. Hand and shoe form opposite supports; NO viewer-left arm NO viewer-right leg. M4: ONLY arm on VIEWER RIGHT, ONLY leg on VIEWER RIGHT; viewer-left side has neither arm nor leg. Hand on viewer-right touches pedestal, only bent leg on viewer-right. Do not hide a supposed second limb behind torso; it must truly not exist. BOTTOM ROW T1 T2 T3 T4 have ALL FOUR LIMBS PRESENT. Head chest abdomen ALWAYS present in every panel. T1: head injury, head droops sideways, small matte maroon forehead scuff, body stands. T2: chest injury, shoulders drawn down, right mitten holds upper chest with subtle upper-sweater maroon scuff, slight upper-torso slump. T3: abdomen injury, clear waist bend and both hands holding lower belly, subtle lower sweater scuff. T4: combined head/chest/abdomen injury, low deep crouch on both legs, waist curled, head bowed, subtle localized marks at all three places. No new equipment or bandages, no glowing effect, no removed torso/head, no blood. These are pose design references not sprite sheets. Prioritize exact visible limb counts and strict top row left/right position over aesthetic variation.
